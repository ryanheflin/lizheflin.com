FAQ

Questions before you join?

Where is The Inkwell hosted?

The community is hosted on Heartbeat, which you can access on desktop or mobile.

What if I miss a live event?

Many sessions and resources are available afterward so you can catch up when it works for you.

What's the refund policy?

The Inkwell includes a 14-day money-back guarantee. If it is not for you, email inkwell@lizheflin.com within that initial 14-day window for a full refund.

Can I cancel anytime?

Monthly members can cancel at any time and keep access until the current month expires. Annual memberships can be canceled for future renewal, but refunds are not available after the initial 14-day guarantee period.

Is this only for new freelance writers?

No. The Inkwell is designed for writers at different stages who want useful conversation, support, and business perspective.
